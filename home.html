Purpose

Australian farmers across the Murray-Darling Basin experience very different levels of water security depending on where they farm, the water entitlements they hold and how much water is available each year. The Farmer's Basin brings this together to show how water security affects farmers across the Basin.

Why it Matters

The Basin underpins a major share of Australian agriculture, but the water it supplies is not shared evenly. When allocations fall, some regions feel it first and hardest.

8,400 irrigated agricultural businesses depend on the Basin Source: Murray-Darling Basin Authority, 2023
$30B in Basin agricultural output each year Source: Murray-Darling Basin Authority, 2023
37% decline in rice production, driven by reduced water availability Source: Murray-Darling Basin Authority, 2023
